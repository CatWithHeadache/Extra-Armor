# Extra-Armor
Forge 1.20.1 mod adding up to 5 extra slots for Helmets, Chestplates, Leggings, and Boots. Custom mod armors and Mending are supported. Slots are locked by default; unlock them by crafting an item with a Chest in the middle and 4 Iron Ingots on the cross sides. Note: AI-generated code, but entirely envisioned, designed, and balanced by a human.
